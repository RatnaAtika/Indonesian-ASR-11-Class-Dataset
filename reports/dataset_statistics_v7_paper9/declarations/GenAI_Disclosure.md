# Declaration of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies in the Manuscript Preparation Process

> Required by Elsevier whenever generative AI / AI-assisted tools were
> used in drafting the manuscript. Place this section **immediately
> before the References list** of the published article.
>
> The disclosure does **not** apply to basic tools (grammar / spell
> check, reference managers without AI features). If no AI tools were
> used, omit this section entirely.
>
> AI tools must **not** be listed as authors and must **not** be cited
> as authors in the references list.

---

## Active statement (default for this work)

During the preparation of this work the author(s) used **Anthropic
Claude (Sonnet)** in order to: (i) draft, format, and proof-read
sections of the manuscript; (ii) audit the dataset statistics and
figures for ScienceDirect / Elsevier publication-format compliance;
and (iii) generate machine-readable structured supplements
(JSON / YAML).

After using this service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the
content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of
the published article.

No generative AI was used to **create or alter images** in this
manuscript.

---

## Hard rules summarised

- **Do not** list any AI tool as an author or co-author.
- **Do not** cite AI tools in the references list.
- **Verify** every AI-generated reference, fact, and number — Elsevier
  flags AI-fabricated citations as a misconduct issue.
- **Do not** use generative AI to create or alter figures, except where
  AI is itself part of the research method (e.g. medical-imaging
  reconstructions); in that case, name the model + version +
  manufacturer in the Methods section.
- **Do not** disclose research participants' data to a third-party AI
  service without an appropriate data-processing agreement and
  consent.
