# Ethics Statement

> Required for all *Data in Brief* / Elsevier ScienceDirect submissions
> involving human subjects (audio recordings of identifiable voices).

## 1. Human subjects

This study involves audio recordings of **20 adult native-Indonesian
speakers** (10 female, 10 male; ages 19–28) reading aloud a fixed,
non-sensitive set of 209 sentences across 11 sentence-type categories.

## 3. Informed consent

All 20 speakers provided **written informed consent** prior to
recording. The consent form explicitly authorised:

1. Recording of the speaker's voice for research purposes.
2. **Public release** of the audio recordings (not only collection).
3. Inclusion of voice samples in derivative scientific publications,
   including this *Data in Brief* article.
4. Re-use of the audio for training and evaluation of speech
   technologies.

A blank consent form template is retained by the corresponding author.

## 4. Personally identifiable information

- Speakers are referenced by **first name only** (e.g. *M3*, *F3*,
  *F5*) in the metadata. No surnames, dates of birth, addresses,
  ID numbers, or any other PII are released.
- The acoustic content does not include the speaker's full name,
  contact details, or any private content; the script is the same
  fixed list of 209 sentences for all speakers.
- Voice itself is biometric. Speakers were informed of the biometric
  nature of voice prior to consent.

## 5. Use of organs / tissues / clinical data

Not applicable. This is a non-medical, non-clinical speech corpus.

## 6. Maps and jurisdictional claims

The data source location field uses the institution name and
approximate decimal-degree coordinates only. No map figure is
included in the article.

## 7. Read-aloud script copyright

The 209 base sentences were authored *de novo* for this corpus by the
research team. No third-party copyright applies. The script is
released under the same licence as the corpus.

## 8. Synthetic-data declaration

132 of the 102,544 files (0.129 % of the corpus, 0.013 % of the test
set) are **synthetic** Edge-TTS gap-fills. They are explicitly flagged
in the metadata (`is_synthetic = True`, `synthesis_engine = "edge-tts"`)
and in §3.6 of the report. No synthetic file replaces a missing real
recording without disclosure.
