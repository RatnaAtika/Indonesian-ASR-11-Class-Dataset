# Declaration of Competing Interests

> Required for every Elsevier journal submission. Save this content as
> a `.docx` file before uploading to Editorial Manager. Author
> signatures are **not** required.

## Statement (default — no competing interests)

The authors declare that they have no known competing financial
interests or personal relationships that could have appeared to
influence the work reported in this paper.

---

## Alternative statement (if competing interests **do** exist)

If any author has a competing interest to declare (employment,
consultancies, stock ownership, honoraria, paid expert testimony,
patent applications or registrations, research grants, or affiliation
with the journal as an Editor or Advisory Board Member), use the
following pattern instead:

> Author A reports financial support was provided by `<funder>`.
> Author B reports a relationship with `<organisation>` that includes:
> `<consulting / employment / stock / etc.>`.
> Author C is `<role>` of `<journal>`. *Editorial-disclosure footnote
> applies* — the corresponding journal editor handled this submission
> independently of Author C, who had no involvement in the peer-review
> process.

The Elsevier "Other Activities" template text for journal-affiliated
authors is:

> Given their role as `<insert journal role title>`, `<insert your
> name>` had no involvement in the peer-review of this article and has
> no access to information regarding its peer-review. Full
> responsibility for the editorial process for this article was
> delegated to another journal editor.

## Submission file

Save this declaration as `Declaration_of_Competing_Interests.docx`
(`.doc/.docx` only — not `.pdf`, not `.txt`) and upload at the
"Attach/Upload Files" step of Editorial Manager.
